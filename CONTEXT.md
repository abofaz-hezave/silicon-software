# Agency Platform

The public-facing system for Silicon Software, a software agency that introduces and customizes software products and services. Intelligent Briefing is its first product; the portfolio may later expand into additional AI tools and AI sovereignty services.

## Language

**Prospect**:
A marketing agency founder or operational or technical leader considering Silicon Software for a product customization engagement. The prospect is usually the buyer; their client-facing team members are the product users.
_Avoid_: User, lead

**Intelligent Briefing**:
Silicon Software’s first AI product, which guides a marketing team through turning client information into a structured briefing. Briefing creation is its current boundary; downstream workflow and project creation are possible future expansions.
_Avoid_: Demo app, portfolio app, Silicon Software

**Briefing**:
The structured description of agreed client work produced from information supplied by a product user.
_Avoid_: Meeting notes, project

**Briefing template**:
An optional, customer-configurable guide to the canonical briefing model. A product user may fill its fields directly or provide information conversationally instead.
_Avoid_: Required form, briefing

**Briefing assistant**:
The conversational interface that gathers briefing information, identifies missing information, asks the product user follow-up questions, and uses the answers to complete the briefing.
_Avoid_: Template, generic chatbot

**Product preview**:
An anonymous, non-production experience that demonstrates Intelligent Briefing with fixed sample content and accepts no visitor meeting data.
_Avoid_: Live product, customer workspace, demo app

**Customization engagement**:
Paid work that adapts Intelligent Briefing to a client’s context; deployment, ownership, and support terms may be chosen separately for each engagement.
_Avoid_: General software project

**Qualified inquiry**:
An inquiry from a prospect whose business need, budget, and engagement fit justify a conversation with the agency.
_Avoid_: Contact submission, lead

**Qualification form**:
The short initial assessment a prospect submits before the agency decides whether to offer a booking link.
_Avoid_: Contact form, booking form
